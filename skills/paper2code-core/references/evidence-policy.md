# Evidence policy

The target PDF is authoritative for what the target paper reported. A Supporting Source may interpret a concept; it cannot silently fill a missing target-paper setting.

Every material formula, parameter, dataset setting, network component, baseline, and result claim receives a stable Evidence Item with page plus equation, figure, table, or citation locator where applicable. Preserve faithful text, units, confidence, dependencies, provenance, and status.

Use these provenance classes: `paper`, `supporting_source`, `derivation`, `user_choice`, and `empirical_choice`. An ambiguity closes only as `confirmed`, `derived`, `reconstructed`, `empirically_selected`, or `excluded`. Before specification approval every `must` ambiguity is terminal and points to its decision record when one exists.

Extraction has two distinct passes. The Evidence Audit adversarially checks every page and section; equations, figures, tables, and citation dependencies; unit conflicts; contradictions; and unsupported inferences. The dossier stays unapproved while any audit item is untriaged.
