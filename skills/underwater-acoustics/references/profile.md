# Underwater-acoustics profile contract

Evidence and contracts record sample rate, carrier frequency, bandwidth, FFT/subcarrier counts, cyclic prefix, modulation, pilot pattern, signal normalization, complex-to-real mapping, channel tap units, delay/Doppler convention, noise definition, SNR convention, CSI source, equalizer equations, SER denominator, and computational-complexity basis whenever applicable.

Classical ZF, MMSE, and DFE methods need Baseline Contracts with equations, regularization/noise terms, CSI assumptions, boundary handling, and analytic fixtures. Learned networks need tensor shapes, complex representation, layer/unfolding semantics, loss, optimizer, schedule, initialization, train/evaluation distributions, seeds, and parameter-count method.

Scientific Validation checks noiseless identity behavior, known channel inverses, ZF/MMSE limiting cases, time/frequency transformations, power and SNR normalization, shape and unit invariants, complex-to-real round trips, differentiability, and SER against hand-worked symbols.
