# Scientific prototype

Take one decision question, Evidence Item set, and checkable outcome. Work only under `prototypes/` or a temporary branch. Record inputs, environment, command, seeds, outputs, result, limitations, and applicable third-party licenses.

A successful prototype contributes an Evidence Item or Reconstruction Decision; its code never moves directly into production. Return through the approved specification, ticket, TDD, and review path. Stop after recording the answer and updating the linked issue.
